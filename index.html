The Story

Bob has deep roots in Cleveland's Christian music scene. As a young man he played bass in the contemporary Christian rock band Fireworks (Live Fireworks, 1980). He went on to promote events and open local businesses, including The New Orleans Showboat restaurant. Today he prepares taxes at RWS Financial Services and leads the 12-piece Good Mix Music band.

Good Mix Music

A 12-piece Cleveland band Bob founded during the pandemic, playing the hits of the '70s and '80s. Its YouTube videos have drawn over a million views and fans as far away as Manila. Bob has played music since age 15, appearing on five national albums including Amy Grant's second album, and played with B.J. Thomas and with Marty McCall & Fireworks.
